# Voicedesk Product FAQ

## What is Voicedesk?

Voicedesk is Acme's AI call assistant. It joins your phone system, transcribes
calls in real time, and drafts follow-up emails automatically.

## Features

### Real-time transcription

Voicedesk transcribes calls with speaker labels in under a second of latency,
in 14 languages.

### Follow-up drafts

After each call, Voicedesk drafts a follow-up email from the transcript. Drafts
are editable and are never sent without user approval.

## Security

### Data handling

Call audio is processed in memory and deleted after transcription. Transcripts
are stored encrypted at rest (AES-256) in the customer's chosen region.

### SSO and provisioning

SSO (SAML and OIDC) and SCIM provisioning are included in the Enterprise plan
only. Starter and Pro use email/password with optional 2FA.

## Support

Starter gets community support. Pro gets email support with a 1-business-day
SLA. Enterprise gets a dedicated success manager and a 4-hour response SLA.
